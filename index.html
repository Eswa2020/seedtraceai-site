How it works

1. Capture

Take a photo of your seed.

2. Name

Add its indigenous name, its meaning, and how it is grown and used.

3. Share

Add it to the community record, with you named as its keeper. You decide what is public and what stays private.

Communities and elders

Keep indigenous names, meanings and traditions alive for the next generation.

Researchers and seed banks

Learn from farmers' knowledge, with their permission and credit.
